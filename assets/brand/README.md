# Ruiz icon concepts

1. `01-knowledge-loop.svg` - Knowledge blueprint plus spaced-repetition loop.
2. `02-recall-r.svg` - Ruiz monogram plus a repeated-recall orbit.
3. `03-knowledge-blueprint.svg` - Imported material becoming connected knowledge units.
4. `04-review-cards.svg` - Review cards plus scheduling loop.
5. `05-review-orbit.svg` - Complete two-color orbit around a study card.
6. `06-review-cadence.svg` - Growing intervals along a review timeline.
7. `07-card-flip.svg` - Active recall shown as question and answer card faces.
8. `08-mastery-ring.svg` - A large card focused on review progress and mastery.
9. `09-review-deck.svg` - A stronger layered-card silhouette with a dark loop.
10. `10-review-minimal.svg` - Simplified card and loop for small icon sizes.

Open `review-variants-preview.svg` to compare concepts 4 through 10 on one canvas.

All concepts use a 512 x 512 view box, flat colors, and a 112-unit corner radius. They are exploratory assets and are not wired into the application.
